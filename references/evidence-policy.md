# Evidence and privacy policy

## Three different claims

Keep these claims separate:

1. **Sponsor claim:** the VC Village NYC event page says Goodwin sponsored the event.
2. **Identity claim:** the LinkedIn URL and official Goodwin biography refer to the same named professional and current firm.
3. **Extraction claim:** Context.dev returned an HTTP response for the exact known LinkedIn URL and the run saved a receipt.

The first two can be validated from public sources. They never substitute for the third.

## Bundled public validation set

The sample contains three public Goodwin professional profiles:

- Andrew Pusar
- Anthony McCusker
- Pat Mitchell

Goodwin's sponsor status is firm-level. The sample makes no claim that these individuals sponsored, hosted, attended, registered for, or endorsed VC Village.

## Public-safe field policy

Allow:

- name;
- current professional title and firm;
- professional location at city/region level when publicly stated;
- practice or domain focus;
- public professional profile and employer-bio URLs;
- evidence status, checked date, and provider receipt metadata; and
- visualization metrics derived from field coverage and evidence quality.

Exclude:

- personal or work email addresses;
- telephone numbers;
- street or home addresses;
- family, age, birth date, protected traits, health, religion, politics, or private affiliations;
- inferred intent, relationship strength, or event attendance;
- authentication cookies and tokens; and
- raw provider responses from the public repository.
